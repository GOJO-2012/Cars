Automotive Chronicle
1886 – 1910s

The Dawn of Motion

In 1886, Karl Benz patented the Motorwagen, widely regarded as the first modern automobile. Powered by a single-cylinder four-stroke engine, it forever changed human transport.

1920s – 1950s

Mass Production & Muscle

Henry Ford revolutionized manufacturing with the moving assembly line for the Model T. Automobiles transitioned from luxury toys for the rich to essential everyday tools.

1960s – 1990s

The Supercar Era

Aerodynamic design, turbochargers, and high-performance engineering took center stage. Iconic names like Ferrari, Lamborghini, and Porsche redefined speed and style.

2000s – Present

Electric & Autonomous

The modern era focuses on sustainability and intelligence. High-density batteries, electric drivetrains, and AI-assisted driving lead the next transformation.
